Features

Everything you need to shorten, brand, and track your links.

  • Link shortening

    Turn long URLs into short links that are easy to share and remember. Tracking parameters are trimmed automatically, so every link stays clean.

  • Branded links

    Use a custom domain and slug so every link points back to your brand. Point your own domain at Shortly and build links that look like part of your site.

  • Click analytics

    See clicks, referrers, and locations over time for every short link. Break results down by day, device, and country, then export them for reports.

  • Team workspaces

    Share links and reports with the people you work with in one place. Invite teammates with roles that keep a shared history of everything published.

  • API access

    Create and manage links programmatically with the Shortly API. Authenticate with a single key, then create, update, and delete links from your own scripts.

  • Fraud protection

    Suspicious clicks are filtered out so your reports stay accurate. Bot traffic and repeat offenders are quietly discarded from your click counts.
