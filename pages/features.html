Features

Everything you need to shorten, brand, and track your links.

  • Link shortening

    Turn long URLs into short links that are easy to share and remember.

  • Branded links

    Use a custom domain and slug so every link points back to your brand.

  • Click analytics

    See clicks, referrers, and locations over time for every short link.

  • Team workspaces

    Share links and reports with the people you work with in one place.

  • API access

    Create and manage links programmatically with the Shortly API.

  • Fraud protection

    Suspicious clicks are filtered out so your reports stay accurate.
